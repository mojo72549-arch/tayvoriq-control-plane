# TAYVORIQ Growth V2 Automation

Status: Draft rollout, production main unchanged until verification.

## Daily operating model

- Two editorial opportunities every day, seven days per week.
- Every slot starts with fresh grounded trend research.
- An active series never bypasses trend research and has no reserved slot.
- Series episodes compete with fresh candidates and may win only on current Growth + Binding performance.
- Telegram remains the human trend-selection gate.
- After trend approval, the existing autonomous production/self-heal/quality path remains authoritative.
- Final YouTube publication approval remains the existing separate Telegram video-approval gate.

## Platform-native output

One approved story produces separate platform variants from one verified fact core:

- YouTube: compact reach-first cut, normally 28–45 seconds for trend stories and 42–70 seconds for deeper explainers.
- TikTok: deeper native cut, normally 65–85 seconds for trend stories and 80–95 seconds for explainers; final runtime must exceed 61 seconds.
- Longer TikTok duration must come from useful verified mechanism/context/consequence, never filler.

## Growth selection

Base quality remains a prerequisite. Growth V2 then ranks current candidates by reach potential, binding potential and combined priority. 10k/20k/50k/100k are optimization targets, never guaranteed outcomes.

## Timing learning

Initial selection windows are 06:30 and 15:30 Europe/Berlin so there is production lead time. Initial publication-window hypotheses are only bootstrap priors. TAYVORIQ learns platform/day/slot performance from its own 2h/24h/72h/168h observations. A slot is not promoted before at least six comparable observations. Breaking/local freshness can override a clock window.

## Safety

No growth change may weaken source verification, voice, visuals, captions, render integrity, final audit, novelty/dedup, approval or publication authorization.

# Growth V2.1 – Additive Learning, Winner and Creative Loop

This section extends Growth V2 only. It does not replace, weaken or reorder the existing Golden Path, Telegram gates, quality gates, recovery rules or publication authorization.

## Core objective

TAYVORIQ must optimize for a repeatable system that produces more strong experiments and learns from every published Short. The objective is not to force a single viral video. The objective is to steadily increase the probability of 10k/20k/50k/100k+ outcomes through measured iteration.

Production reliability and content learning are treated as two separate but connected systems:

1. Production reliability must maximize the number of publishable opportunities without lowering quality.
2. Growth learning must use real platform performance to improve the next trend ranking, hook, structure, format and timing decision.

A production failure is not a content-performance signal. A content underperformance is not automatically a production-quality failure.

## Learning observations

For every published platform variant, collect comparable observations at the existing 2h/24h/72h/168h checkpoints whenever the platform exposes the required data.

The learning record should retain at minimum:

- platform
- publication timestamp and slot
- topic and topic cluster
- trend freshness at selection/publication
- content angle
- series/non-series marker
- hook family
- opening visual family
- runtime
- pacing/cut profile
- CTA family
- views/reach where available
- watch/retention signals where available
- average watch duration where available
- average percentage viewed/completion where available
- rewatch/loop signal where available
- likes, comments, shares and saves where available
- subscriber/follower conversion where available
- data completeness and observation age

Missing platform metrics must be marked as unavailable. They must never be invented or replaced by guessed values.

## Relative baseline before absolute targets

TAYVORIQ evaluates a Short against comparable recent TAYVORIQ content first, not against arbitrary creator benchmarks.

Comparable means, where enough data exists:

- same platform
- similar content class: trend / explainer / series / local / business / technology / sport / world event
- similar runtime band
- similar publication slot or daypart
- similar observation age

10k/20k/50k/100k remain useful outcome milestones, but optimization decisions are based on relative uplift against TAYVORIQ's own rolling baseline.

## Winner state model

A video must not be called a winner from views alone or from an immature observation.

Suggested states:

- LEARNING_PENDING: insufficient observation age/data.
- EARLY_SIGNAL: promising relative performance at 2h, not yet confirmed.
- WINNER_CANDIDATE: material relative uplift across multiple meaningful signals.
- WINNER_CONFIRMED: uplift remains strong at 24h/72h with sufficient data.
- REPEATABLE_PATTERN: the same hook/angle/format family performs strongly across multiple different topics.
- FALSE_POSITIVE: early spike did not persist or was explained by a one-off external event.

One successful video may inspire the next experiment but must not permanently rewrite the strategy by itself.

## Creative experiment discipline

TAYVORIQ should learn as much as possible from two daily opportunities without turning the channel into random experimentation.

Rules:

- Prefer changing one major creative variable at a time when testing a hypothesis.
- Do not repeatedly upload near-duplicate versions of the same story just to test hooks.
- Keep the verified fact core stable while testing presentation variables.
- Do not change topic, source context or factual meaning to manufacture a stronger hook.
- Record which variable was intentionally tested.
- Do not promote a pattern from one result alone.

Major testable variables include:

- hook wording/family
- opening visual
- first-scene speed
- question vs. consequence vs. surprise opening
- explanation density
- scene-change cadence
- runtime band
- CTA family
- series framing

## Hook strategy

Every production should have a deliberate first-1.5-second strategy before full rendering.

A strong TAYVORIQ hook should normally contain at least one of:

- consequence: why this matters now
- conflict: what changed or what is at stake
- surprise: the non-obvious fact
- curiosity gap: a concrete unanswered question
- direct relevance: what it means for Germany/the viewer/the affected group

Avoid generic openings that merely restate the headline.

The hook may be more compelling than the source headline, but never more certain, sensational or factual than the verified evidence allows.

## TAYVORIQ format families

Growth learning should group content into recognizable reusable families instead of treating every video as unrelated.

Initial families may include:

- WAS BEDEUTET DAS JETZT? – consequence-first current event explanation
- DAS ÜBERSIEHT FAST JEDER – non-obvious consequence/context
- WARUM PASSIERT DAS? – mechanism explainer
- IN 45 SEKUNDEN VERSTANDEN – compressed explainer
- TAYVORIQ SERIE – recurring multi-part explanatory arc

These are strategy families, not mandatory literal titles. A family stays active only if real TAYVORIQ performance supports it.

## Trend ranking extension

The existing fresh grounded trend research remains mandatory. Growth ranking should additionally consider:

- freshness
- audience relevance
- consequence strength
- curiosity/surprise potential
- conflict/change signal
- visual potential
- explainability in the target runtime
- TAYVORIQ historical performance of the topic cluster
- TAYVORIQ historical performance of the proposed hook/format family
- novelty versus recently published TAYVORIQ content
- series continuity value when applicable

Historical performance may influence ranking but must never override evidence quality, freshness, novelty/dedup or editorial relevance by itself.

## Opening-visual rule

The first visual must support the hook immediately. It may not be a generic logo intro, unrelated stock footage or a visually calm placeholder when a stronger verified/appropriate visual exists.

Visual selection should prefer:

1. direct subject/event visual
2. strong contextual visual tied to the exact claim
3. explanatory graphic/map/data visual when it improves comprehension
4. generic stock only when it is semantically correct and no better option exists

The existing visual quality and factual-match gates remain authoritative.

## Voice identity

The TAYVORIQ voice should be treated as a brand component, not merely a TTS dependency.

Growth requirements do not alter the existing native/natural voice quality gate. Learning may compare pacing, emphasis and sentence structure, but it must not introduce an artificial, robotic or "anonymous" delivery style simply to increase speed.

Pronunciation regressions that have already been understood must remain protected by regression tests or deterministic pronunciation rules.

## Failure-to-regression rule

The existing Golden Path rule remains: every recurring production error that is understood must become a regression guard, preflight check, deterministic fallback or documented project lesson.

Growth V2.1 adds one operational objective:

A known technical failure must not repeatedly consume complete creative experiments if it can be detected before expensive production or isolated to a local repair path.

Where technically appropriate, use lightweight preflight/canary checks for critical external dependencies before expensive generation. This must not duplicate or bypass the authoritative Golden Path technical preflight.

## Bounded recovery protects learning throughput

Recovery must remain bounded and local. The system must not create artificial "more experiments" by regenerating new content during recovery.

The approved request remains immutable. Good verified assets remain reusable. A failed Voice/Visual/Subtitle/Render/Audit component should be repaired locally according to Golden Path policy.

Growth statistics must count published content opportunities, not recovery generations, retries or failed technical attempts.

## 30-day operating target

The preferred operating target is up to two high-quality publishable opportunities per day, subject to the existing approval and Golden Path constraints.

For a healthy 30-day learning cycle, TAYVORIQ should aim to produce enough clean observations to answer:

- Which topic clusters create the strongest retention and engagement?
- Which hook families reduce early drop-off?
- Which opening visuals correlate with stronger starts?
- Which runtime bands work by platform and content class?
- Which format families are repeatable rather than one-off hits?
- Which publication windows work for TAYVORIQ's own audience?
- Which series deserve continuation?

The target is learning density, not quota pressure. Quality gates are never weakened to hit a daily or monthly number.

## Promotion and rollback policy

A learned optimization is promoted only when supported by enough comparable observations.

Promotion rules:

- Prefer repeated evidence over one viral outlier.
- Preserve the previous known-good configuration so a change can be rolled back.
- Treat new growth weights as bounded adjustments, not total strategy rewrites.
- Do not automatically suppress entire topic classes from one weak result.
- Do not automatically copy a winning hook verbatim across unrelated stories.
- If a promoted change degrades comparable performance, roll back the growth change without touching Golden Path quality controls.

## Separation of authority

Growth intelligence may recommend or score:

- trend priority
- hook family
- format family
- runtime band
- opening visual strategy
- timing hypothesis
- CTA family

Growth intelligence may not weaken or override:

- source verification
- fact lock
- request lock
- novelty/dedup
- voice quality
- visual factual match
- captions
- render integrity
- final quality audit
- Telegram approval rules
- publication authorization
- Golden Path recovery ownership

## Success definition

TAYVORIQ Growth V2.1 is successful when the system demonstrates all three simultaneously:

1. Reliable throughput: fewer repeated technical failures consume full production opportunities.
2. Measurable learning: each published Short contributes structured comparable performance data.
3. Repeatable growth: multiple hook/format/topic combinations outperform the rolling baseline across more than one video.

A 100k+ video is a milestone. A system that can repeatedly identify and reproduce stronger patterns is the actual product goal.
