# TAYVORIQ Growth V2 Automation

Status: Draft rollout, production main unchanged until verification.

## Daily operating model

- Two editorial opportunities every day, seven days per week.
- Every slot starts with fresh grounded trend research.
- An active series never bypasses trend research and has no reserved slot.
- Series episodes compete with fresh candidates and may win only on current Growth + Binding performance.
- Telegram remains the human trend-selection gate.
- After trend approval, the existing autonomous production/self-heal/quality path remains authoritative.
- Final YouTube publication approval remains the existing separate Telegram video-approval gate.

## Platform-native output

One approved story produces separate platform variants from one verified fact core:

- YouTube: compact reach-first cut, normally 28–45 seconds for trend stories and 42–70 seconds for deeper explainers.
- TikTok: deeper native cut, normally 65–85 seconds for trend stories and 80–95 seconds for explainers; final runtime must exceed 61 seconds.
- Longer TikTok duration must come from useful verified mechanism/context/consequence, never filler.

## Growth selection

Base quality remains a prerequisite. Growth V2 then ranks current candidates by reach potential, binding potential and combined priority. 10k/20k/50k/100k are optimization targets, never guaranteed outcomes.

## Timing learning

Initial selection windows are 06:30 and 15:30 Europe/Berlin so there is production lead time. Initial publication-window hypotheses are only bootstrap priors. TAYVORIQ learns platform/day/slot performance from its own 2h/24h/72h/168h observations. A slot is not promoted before at least six comparable observations. Breaking/local freshness can override a clock window.

## Safety

No growth change may weaken source verification, voice, visuals, captions, render integrity, final audit, novelty/dedup, approval or publication authorization.
