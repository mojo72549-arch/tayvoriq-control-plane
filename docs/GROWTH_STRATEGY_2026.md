# TAYVORIQ Growth Strategy 2026

Status: implementation baseline for YouTube + TikTok. Instagram is intentionally deferred until both core channels run reliably.

## 1. Objective

TAYVORIQ is optimized for two outcomes in parallel:

1. reach: win new viewers through current, searchable, visually strong stories;
2. return: convert viewers into subscribers/followers through coherent series and recurring formats.

The system does **not** increase topic volume blindly. It researches two strong stories per day and creates platform-specific variants from the same verified evidence package.

## 2. Daily operating model

- Morning = **Reach slot**: current event/trend with high hook, search and share potential.
- Evening = **Return slot**: active series episode or recurring explainer designed to create a reason to come back.
- Two researched stories/day remain the editorial maximum by default.
- Each approved story can produce:
  - YouTube variant: target 40-55 s, ideal ~50 s;
  - TikTok variant: target 65-85 s, ideal ~72 s.
- The TikTok extension must add useful explanation or a second payoff. Filler is forbidden.

Result: 2 researched stories/day -> up to 2 YouTube Shorts + 2 TikTok videos/day, without researching four separate topics.

## 3. YouTube growth target

Current platform reference (must be reverified before automated eligibility decisions):

- Early expanded YPP: 500 subscribers + 3 valid public uploads in 90 days + 3,000,000 valid public Shorts views in 90 days.
- Shorts ad revenue tier: 1,000 subscribers + 10,000,000 valid public Shorts views in 90 days.

At exactly two YouTube Shorts/day over 90 days (180 Shorts):

- 3,000,000 / 180 ~= 16,667 views per Short average, ~= 33,333 channel Shorts views/day.
- 10,000,000 / 180 ~= 55,556 views per Short average, ~= 111,111 channel Shorts views/day.

These are planning averages, not per-video requirements. Viral winners can compensate for weaker videos.

Primary YouTube optimization sequence:

Hook -> retention -> payoff -> rewatch/share -> subscriber conversion.

## 4. TikTok growth target

Current Creator Rewards reference (must be reverified before automated eligibility decisions):

- 10,000 followers;
- 100,000 video views in the previous 30 days;
- eligible original videos must be at least one minute long.

At two TikTok uploads/day (60 videos/30 days), 100,000 total views equals ~1,667 views/video average or ~3,333 channel views/day. The follower target is therefore treated as a first-class KPI, not an afterthought.

Primary TikTok optimization sequence:

Hook -> sustained watch time -> second payoff -> share/search value -> follow conversion.

## 5. Series contract

A series is a growth mechanism, not a reason to make repetitive videos.

Every episode must:

- be understandable without seeing the previous episode;
- open with the strongest curiosity/fact hook, never with "Teil X";
- show series identity only after attention is earned;
- answer one distinct factual question;
- use the global duplicate gate;
- end with a factual bridge to the next episode;
- use a specific CTA tied to the next useful payoff.

Example CTA pattern: "Im nächsten Teil zeigen wir, warum genau daraus ein Tsunami entstehen kann."

## 6. Trend ranking upgrade

Editorial quality remains mandatory. A candidate is additionally evaluated for:

- hook_strength;
- retention_potential;
- follow_conversion_potential;
- series_fit;
- share_comment_potential;
- search_discoverability.

Final ranking target:

- 60% editorial quality;
- 40% growth potential.

Source quality remains a gate. A viral-looking candidate with weak evidence must not win.

## 7. Measurement loop

For every published platform variant record, when available:

- views;
- first-3-second hold;
- average view duration;
- average percentage viewed;
- completion rate;
- rewatch rate;
- shares/1,000 views;
- comments/1,000 views;
- subscribers or follows/1,000 views;
- returning viewers;
- next-episode continuation for series;
- TikTok qualified views after Creator Rewards eligibility.

Do not hard-code generic creator-industry benchmarks as truth. After at least 30 variants, use the channel's own rolling baseline and promote formats that repeatedly beat it.

## 8. Rollout order

1. Preserve existing approval, evidence, quality and duplicate gates.
2. Add growth scoring to the trend/series dossier.
3. Split YouTube and TikTok duration/script profiles while reusing the evidence package.
4. Add per-platform analytics feedback to future trend ranking.
5. Only after YouTube + TikTok are stable, add Instagram as a third platform profile.

## 9. Non-goals

- no bought views, followers or engagement;
- no four-topic-per-day content mill;
- no repeated topic with cosmetic wording changes;
- no quality-gate weakening for speed;
- no artificial one-minute padding for TikTok.
